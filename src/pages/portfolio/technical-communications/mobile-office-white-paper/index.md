# Mobile White Paper

## Overview

This white paper demonstrates research-driven technical communication developed to address a business need, translate complex IT topics for a professional audience, and present recommendations in a clear, persuasive format.

## Project Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| Audience | IT professionals |
| Document Type | White Paper |
| Industry | Information Technology |
| Tools | Microsoft Word |
| Skills | Research, Technical Writing, Content Strategy, Editing, Information Design|

## Challenge

The client wanted a white paper that would demonstrate the IT department's competitiveness and thought leadership but provided only a general project scope. The project did not have a predetermined topic, established content framework, or visual identity, giving me responsibility for developing the direction of the document.

## Solution

I researched the IT department's priorities and emerging technology topics and identified workplace mobile-device adoption as a timely subject. After the client approved the direction, I developed the content plan, researched the topic, wrote the white paper, and created a professional visual presentation for the document. 

## Result

The proposed topic and content plan were approved by the client, resulting in a 15-page white paper that I researched, wrote, edited, and designed from concept through final delivery.
